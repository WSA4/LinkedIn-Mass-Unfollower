## 📌 **What is this project?**  
LinkedIn is a great platform, but let’s be honest—your feed is probably full of posts you **don’t care about**.   

This **Firefox extension automates unfollowing** on LinkedIn. No more **manual clicking** to clean up your feed. This tool **unfollows everyone** (or specific groups) with just one click.  

🔥 **One click. Less noise. More relevant content.**  

---

## 🤔 **Why is this useful?**  
I built this because I felt frustrated with how **painfully slow and annoying it was to unfollow people one-by-one on LinkedIn**.  

✅ **Cleans up your feed** without deleting connections  
✅ **Unfollows everyone in one go** (or just specific groups)  
✅ **Automatically scrolls down** to find more connections  
✅ **Avoids LinkedIn bot detection** by leaving 9 people followed  

So instead of just seeing more **spammy posts** from self-proclaimed "thought leaders,"  you can can customise your feed to see the updates that actually matter to you.

## 🛠 **How to Install**  
**Instead of reloading it manually every time, follow these steps to install it permanently:**  

### 🔹 **Install the Extension Manually**  
1. **Download the ZIP file** from [GitHub Releases]().  
2. **Extract the ZIP file** to a folder on your computer.  
3. **Open Firefox Developer Mode:**  
   - Open **Firefox**  
   - Type **`about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox`** in the address bar  
4. Click **"Load Temporary Add-on"** and select **`manifest.json`** from the extracted folder.  
5. The extension should now be active in your toolbar.  

💡 **Permanent Solution:**  
1. Open **`about:config`** in Firefox.  
2. Search for `"xpinstall.signatures.required"` and set it to `false`.  
3. Now you can permanently install unsigned extensions.

## 🚀 **How to Use**  
1️⃣ Open **Firefox**   
2️⃣ Click the extension icon.  
3️⃣ Choose:  
   - **Unfollow Following** → Unfollows people you chose to follow  
   - **Unfollow Followers** → Unfollows people LinkedIn automatically adds to your feed when you accept or add new contacts  
4️⃣ Watch the magic happen! The extension will:  
   - **Unfollow users one by one** (LinkedIn doesn’t allow bulk actions, so its a bit of a slow process)  
   - **Automatically scrolls down and loads more users to unfollow**  
   - **Slow-ish unfollow and leaving a few (9) users followed** to prevent LinkedIn from flagging your account  
5️⃣ Once done, you can choose to unfollow the other group.
