LinkedIn Mass Unfollower

What it Does

Automatically unfollow all your LinkedIn connections in either the 'Following' or 'Followers' sections.

How it Works

  1. We will redirect you to the appropriate LinkedIn page.
  2. It will securely automate unfollowing users one by one.
  3. It first checks if there are any people you follow, then scrolls down accordingly.
  4. Wait times are enforced to ensure LinkedIn loads fully.
  5. You'll have the option to continue with the other section.

Important Notes

  • Keep the browser and page open while it runs.
  • At least a few users will remain visible to avoid extreme bot detection flags.
